About Us

Quality accessory supply for the transport industry

Alkur Ticaret, located in the Ikitelli Organized Industrial Zone in Istanbul, supplies ropes, rubbers, eyelets, hooks, buckles, webbing and reflectors for truck, trailer and lorry tarpaulin systems.

With our registered brand SUPER TIR Cable we sell customs ropes at home and abroad, and with our wide range we meet every need of tarpaulin makers, trailer manufacturers and transport companies.

Our principle is to deliver the right product, at the right price, on time. Most of our products ship from stock, and we offer special pricing for wholesale and export orders.

  • Wide product range, fast delivery from stock
  • Galvanized, stainless steel and brass material options
  • Competitive pricing for wholesale and export orders
  • Canvas & trailer hooks in many types and sizes
