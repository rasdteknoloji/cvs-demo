Product groups · 01

Trucks and lorries

A thousand kilometres on the road, not a drop missing from the tank. The aluminium strainer body fitted to tractor, truck and tipper tanks takes the fuel nozzle in; hoses, siphons and jerrycans stay out. At rest stops, overnight parking and driver changes, the fuel stays where it belongs.

Coaches, midibuses and city buses

Every trip starts with a full tank and ends with a full account. On coaches, midibuses, city buses and shuttle vehicles, driver changes, terminal waits and overnight parking are the riskiest moments; with CVS the tank stays locked through all of them, and the key stays with fleet management.

Product groups · 03

Construction machinery

The site sleeps, the machine waits, the fuel stays put. Excavator, loader, grader, roller and asphalt-machine tanks get an aluminium body built for dust, mud and vibration. Even on an empty site at the weekend, the tank stays locked.

Product groups · 04

Concrete mixers and pumps

On site by day, at the plant by night; mixers and pumps never stand idle, and neither do their tanks. Fitted to transit-mixer and concrete-pump tanks, the CVS system lets only the fuel nozzle reach the fuel, at every pour and every wait. The tank that leaves the plant comes back at the same level.
