Our reference projects

The same discipline for every fleet, a solution tailored to the vehicle.

Our work with reference companies does not end with a one-off sale. We examine the structure of the fleet together, choose the system that suits the vehicle type, and remain at their side with support after installation. The process follows these steps at every company.

Start the process

  1. Needs analysis and survey

    The vehicle types in the fleet, the number of tanks and the points where fuel loss occurs are determined together with the company. Vehicles are inspected on site when necessary.

  2. Model selection to suit the vehicle

    The aluminium body and installation-kit parts matching each make and model are specified; the company receives a clear quote.

  3. Installation and sealing

    Every fuel access point from the tank neck to the diesel filter valve is closed and sealed.

  4. Training and handover

    Fleet managers and drivers are shown the correct use and maintenance of the system; key management is handed over to the fleet manager.

  5. After-sales support

    As the fleet grows, the same system is applied to new vehicles. We stay by the company’s side for technical support and spare-part needs.
