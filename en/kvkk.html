Privacy Notice

Your information stays with us. We use the details you give us for quotes and contact only to get back to you; we never sell, rent or share them for advertising.

In short: when you fill in the form, we use your name, company, phone number and vehicle details only to prepare your quote and get in touch. This information stays with us.

2. Which data do we process?

  • Identity and contact: name, company name, phone number, email address (if you write to us).
  • Request details: vehicle type, number of vehicles and any notes you send with your quote request.
  • Communication records: your correspondence with us via WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Technical data: records such as IP address, browser type and access time kept by our hosting provider for security purposes when you visit the site.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (health, biometric data, etc.) through our site; please do not enter such information in the forms.

3. For which purposes do we process your data?

  • To receive and evaluate your quote request and present you with a price and installation plan,
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email,
  • To run our sales, installation, training and after-sales support processes,
  • To fulfil our legal obligations and protect our legal rights in possible disputes,
  • To keep the site secure and prevent misuse.

4. Legal grounds

Your personal data is processed, by automated or non-automated means, on the following legal grounds set out in Article 5 of the KVKK:

  • Being directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c): quotation and sales processes,
  • Fulfilment of the data controller’s legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç),
  • Establishment, exercise or protection of a right (Art. 5/2-e),
  • Our legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5/2-f): site security and service quality.

Commercial electronic messages such as campaign and information messages are sent only if you give separate explicit consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

5. To whom may your data be transferred?

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for advertising. Only to the extent necessary for the purposes above:

  • Our authorised installation teams and business partners,
  • Advisers from whom we receive accounting, legal and similar services,
  • Authorised public institutions and bodies, upon request,
  • The technical service providers that host our site and provide our email service

may be transferred. The servers of our hosting and form service providers may be located abroad; such transfers are made in accordance with the conditions set out in Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. How long do we keep it?

Quote requests are kept for a maximum of 2 years from the conclusion of the request; records that turn into sales are kept for the periods required by the relevant legislation (for example 10 years for commercial books and documents). When the period expires, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Your rights under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, by applying to us you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
  • Know the third parties to whom your data is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • To request the correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, its deletion or destruction when the conditions are met, and the notification of these actions to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • Object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  • Request compensation for damage suffered due to unlawful processing

rights.

8. How to contact us

You can submit your requests in writing to the address above together with information verifying your identity, or send an email to koruma@cvsyakitkoruma.com. Your application is concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller.
