Cookie Policy

We use no advertising or tracking cookies on our site. We only store in your browser the small preferences needed for the site to work properly.

Contents 1. What is a cookie?2. What do we store?3. Third parties4. You are in control5. Contact Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. What is a cookie?

Cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) are small pieces of information saved in your browser when you visit a site. They allow the site to remember your preferences.

2. What do we store on our site?

NamePurposeTypeDuration
cvs-themeRemembers your dark / light mode preference.Essential · local storageUntil you delete it

These contain no personal data and are never sent to any server. Our site has no advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies. Fonts are also served from the site’s own server.

3. Third-party services

The installation video is served through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only when you press play. While the video is playing, YouTube may use its own cookies. When you click WhatsApp or social media links, the policies of those platforms apply.

4. You are in control

You can delete or block cookies and site data at any time from your browser settings. The site will continue to work; only your theme preference will be forgotten.
