Together, always better

One of the pioneers in the fight against fuel theft in Türkiye.

Get a fleet quote

CVS Fuel Protection Systems operates as a pioneering company in the fight against fuel theft in Türkiye. It offers an effective solution against diesel/fuel theft in every kind of diesel-powered vehicle and equipment, including commercial vehicles, construction machinery, site vehicles, forklifts, generators, lorries and trucks.

With products developed entirely with domestic resources, CVS Fuel Protection Systems provides its customers with safe and economical fuel use. Diesel/fuel theft is a major problem, especially for commercial vehicle owners, and can result in high costs. That is why customers need to bring the diesel/fuel consumption of their vehicles under control.

Thanks to the solutions offered by CVS Fuel Protection Systems, customers can prevent diesel/fuel theft and bring the fuel consumption of their vehicles under control. With products designed specifically for its customers, the company continuously monitors diesel/fuel consumption, detects theft immediately and takes the necessary measures.

We always put the satisfaction and safety of our customers first.

Alongside its products, CVS Fuel Protection Systems also provides reliable after-sales services. The company offers technical support and training to its customers and informs them about the correct use and maintenance of its products.

In conclusion, CVS Fuel Protection Systems stands out as a company that offers an effective solution against fuel theft and provides its customers with safe and economical fuel use through products developed with domestic resources. Putting customer satisfaction and safety first, the company holds a pioneering position in the industry.

Our origins

Fuel security backed by more than twenty years of automotive experience.

CVS Fuel Protection Systems was founded to manufacture automotive service equipment and fuel protection security systems, alongside automotive spare-parts production, service and maintenance. Specialised for more than 20 years in spare parts, service, maintenance and repair for heavy commercial, light commercial and passenger vehicles, CVS has brought this expertise to fuel security in heavy commercial vehicles.

Our fundamental goal is to keep customer, employee and supplier satisfaction at the highest level and to be an organisation built on continuity and reliability, responsible towards society and the environment. We run our quality management system with the participation of all our employees and the support of management.
