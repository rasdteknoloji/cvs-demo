Privacy Policy

We protect your information just as we protect your tank. Every detail you give us stays with us and is used only for your request.

Contents 1. Our promise2. Information we collect3. How we use it4. Sharing5. Security6. Links7. Changes8. Contact Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Our promise

Your information stays with us.We use what you write in the quote form only to get back to you.
We do not sell or rent.We never sell your personal information to any company or add it to advertising lists.
No unsolicited messages.We never send campaign or advertising messages without your consent.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to provide any information to browse the site. Only when you fill in the quote form or contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email do we receive your name, company, phone number, vehicle type and number of vehicles. The servers hosting the site may briefly keep visit records (IP address, browser type, time of visit) for security purposes.

3. How do we use your information?

  • To identify the right product for your fleet and prepare your quote,
  • To schedule survey, installation and training appointments,
  • To respond to your after-sales support and spare-part requests,
  • To fulfil our legal obligations.

4. Who do we share your information with?

Your information is shared only with those who need it to do the job: our authorised installation teams, our accounting and legal advisers, the technical providers that run our website and email, and public authorities where required by law. For details, see our Privacy Notice.

5. How do we protect your information?

The site connection is encrypted with SSL (the padlock icon in the address bar). Only our authorised employees can access quote records. We do not keep data longer than necessary and delete expired records. Although no transfer over the internet can be guaranteed to be one hundred percent secure, we take all reasonable technical and administrative measures.

6. Links to other sites

Our site contains links to YouTube, WhatsApp and our social media accounts. When you switch to these services, the privacy policies of the respective companies apply. The installation video does not load until you press play.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page together with the date of the last update.

8. Contact

For any question about privacy, write to koruma@cvsyakitkoruma.com or call us on +90 212 438 20 34.
